The Riella Way

Gabriella

Founder & Creative Mind — Riella Luxé

Gabriella is the founder and creative mind behind Riella Luxé, an emotional scent house exploring the connection between fragrance, feeling and intentional living.

For the past five years, she has been building Riella around a simple belief: we should be intentional about how we want life to feel. What began with fragrance has grown into a broader creative world spanning personal scent, home fragrance and immersive experiences.

The Riella Experience is where that philosophy comes to life.

It is a space for experiences created by Riella and for you — from original concepts like The Mood Table, Scent Sessions and Gallery of Emotions to bespoke mood styling, event styling, sensory styling, ambient scenting, event curation and production.

Whether we’re creating a dinner around a feeling, transforming a space, introducing scent into an environment or designing an entire experience from concept to execution, the question is always the same:

How should this moment feel?

Gabriella is interested in the details that make an experience memorable, because for her, creating something beautiful is only part of the work.

The real magic is in creating something people can feel.
